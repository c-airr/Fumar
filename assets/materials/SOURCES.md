# Stock materials

Ten texture sets from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com), at 2K, as JPG. Every one is
released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain): free to use, change and redistribute,
with no attribution required. They are credited here anyway.

Each folder keeps only the maps fumar reads - colour, OpenGL normal, roughness,
and ambient occlusion and metalness where the set has them. Displacement maps,
preview images and the Blender/USD/MaterialX files that come in the download are
left out. Each folder's `.fmat` names its maps; see `docs/materials.md` on `main`.
